# Workflow interview guide

Use when the user has no files to share, or to fill gaps after analyzing files. The aim is concrete, recent examples — not opinions about what app they would like.

## How to interview

- Ask **two or three questions at a time**, then follow up on what they say.
- Ask for the **last concrete time** something happened: "When did you last do that? What exactly did you do?"
- Ask **how long it took** and **how often** it happens. Record their words; don't round them up.
- Don't suggest solutions during the interview. Collect first, propose later.
- Stop when you have several specific, repeated situations, or after about 15 questions. The user can end it anytime.
- Record each useful answer as an observation with source `interview` and today's date. The user's own statements count as **Observed**.

## Questions

Pick what fits; skip what doesn't.

### Routines

1. Walk me through a normal workday or weekday. Which parts feel repetitive?
2. Is there something you do every week or month that you dread or keep postponing?
3. What do you copy from one place and paste into another?

### Tools

4. Which apps do you open every day? Which devices do you use them on?
5. Which app annoys you most? What were you trying to do when it last did?
6. Are you paying for a subscription you only use one small feature of?
7. Do you keep a spreadsheet, note, or list that you update by hand?

### Information

8. What information do you struggle to find when you need it?
9. What do you keep track of in your head because no tool fits?
10. Do you convert things between formats (files, units, text styles, languages)?

### AI use

11. What do you ask AI tools to do most often? Do you re-explain the same instructions?

### Ideas

12. Have you ever thought "someone should make an app for this"? What was it?
13. Is there something you started automating or building and abandoned? Why?

### Follow-ups (use often)

- "When was the last time? How long did it take?"
- "What happens if you skip it or get it wrong?"
- "How do you handle it today?"
- "Who else is involved?" (record roles, not names)

## After the interview

Summarize the observations back to the user in a short list and ask whether anything is wrong or missing before writing the report.
